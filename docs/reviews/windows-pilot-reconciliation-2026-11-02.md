## 2. Remaining Defects Closed in This Replacement Candidate

1. **Cross-Engine Half-Paise Financial Posting Parity (`packages/business-domain/src/fixed-decimal.ts`, `lib/accounting-posting.ts`, `lib/store.tsx`, `lib/scanning/counter.ts`, `app/(console)/sales/page.tsx`)**
   - Native Rust (`packages/local-store/src/billing.rs`) and `calculateNativeInvoice` (`packages/business-domain/src/native-invoice.ts`) round positive line totals and tax with integer BigInt half-up (`4.85 × ₹0.50 = ₹2.43` / `243` paise; `₹48.50` at `5%` tax = `₹2.43` tax, `₹50.93` total).
   - Previously, `lib/accounting-posting.ts` (`postSale`) and `lib/store.tsx` (`createInvoice` saved item totals) used `lib/accounting.ts::money` (`Math.round((value + Number.EPSILON) * 100) / 100`), posting `₹2.42` (`4.85 * 0.5 * 100 === 242.49999999999997` in IEEE-754) while the invoice preview showed `₹2.43`. Counter `ticketTotal` (`lib/scanning/counter.ts`) and `SalesPage` (`app/(console)/sales/page.tsx`) also summed IEEE-754 floats before rounding.
   - `postSale`, `createInvoice`, `ticketTotal`, and `SalesPage` now compute line totals, subtotal, tax, total, and stock cost adjustments with the exact `QuantityMilli × Paise` BigInt half-up contract.
2. **Stale-Window Inventory Guard Recovery & Native Test Bridge Parity (`apps/desktop/src/InventoryPanel.tsx`, `packages/local-store/examples/cashier_review_bridge.rs`)**
   - `InventoryPanel.tsx` now clears unaccepted `pending` state on `'checkout_active'`, `'forbidden'`, and `'workspace_mismatch'` (in addition to `'invalid_product'`, `'duplicate_sku'`, `'stock_conflict'`, and `'operation_conflict'`), keeping **Reload item counts** enabled when a stale window encounters an already-pending native inventory receipt instead of trapping the operator in a `Retry item save` -> `checkout_active` loop.
   - `cashier_review_bridge.rs` now maps `DuplicateSku -> "duplicate_sku"`, `InvalidProduct | CatalogFull -> "invalid_product"`, `AccountExists -> "account_exists"`, and `AccessConfigured / AccessNotConfigured` identically to `apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/main.rs`.
