## 1. Essential Correctness & Operator-Safety Fixes Applied in This Pass

Following strict release discipline, **only surgical fixes that directly improve financial display accuracy or prevent silent operator traps** were applied to `apps/desktop/src/`:

| Priority | Workflow | Defect Found | Risk at a Real Counter | Surgical Fix Applied |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **P0 — Financial Display Precision** | Stock inward / adjustment (`InventoryPanel.tsx`) & Customer credit (`CustomerPanel.tsx`) | `InventoryPanel.tsx` defined a duplicate `(value / 100).toLocaleString(...)` float helper instead of using `billing-types.ts`'s `BigInt` `money()`, and `CustomerPanel.tsx` restored editable `amountPaise` / `creditLimitPaise` via IEEE-754 float `String(paise / 100)` (dropping trailing zeros like `10.5` and risking drift above `2^53` paise). | Screen inputs and summaries could format paise inconsistently with `CheckoutPanel` and native `i64` paise storage. | Centralized `BigInt` `formatPaiseInput(paise, zeroText)` in `billing-types.ts`, wired `CheckoutPanel.tsx`, `CustomerPanel.tsx`, and `InventoryPanel.tsx` to `billing-types.ts`, and added an AST/source contract check ensuring zero `*Paise / 100` float division anywhere in `apps/desktop/src/`. |
| **P1 — Silent Dead-Click Trap** | Returns/refunds & Daily cash closing corrections (`RetailPanel.tsx`) | Clicking **Review invoice return**, **Review opening float correction**, **Review closing count correction**, or **Review cashier day reopening** when **Return or correction reason** (`#retail-reason`) was empty checked `if (why.trim())` and silently did nothing — no `role="alert"` message and no focus jump, even though `#retail-reason` sits in a lower fieldset far from **Cashier day**. | During a busy counter return or closing correction, the button appears broken ("dead click"), tempting repeated clicks or abandonment. | Added `requireReason()` in `RetailPanel.tsx` to emit an explicit `role="alert"` message (`Enter a return or correction reason before reviewing this action.`) and move focus directly to `#retail-reason`. |
| **P1 — Keyboard Trap & Orphaned Focus on Confirmation Prompts** | Customer credit repayment (`CustomerPanel.tsx`) & Returns / Cashier closing (`RetailPanel.tsx`) | Opening **Review credit entry** or **Review retail operation** unmounted the trigger button without focusing **Confirm credit entry** / **Confirm retail operation** (leaving `document.activeElement` on `document.body`) and ignored the `Escape` key. | Keyboard-first operators had to Tab dozens of times to reach **Confirm** or use the mouse to cancel a mistaken review, unlike `CheckoutPanel` where `Confirm sale` auto-focuses and `Escape` cancels. | Auto-focused `confirmButton` on review entry and bound `Escape` to cancel review cleanly in both `CustomerPanel.tsx` and `RetailPanel.tsx`. |

## 2. Workflow-by-Workflow Inspection Matrix (All 8 Counter Workflows)

| Workflow | Strengths Verified in Current Build | Remaining Friction / Deferred UX Items (Prioritized for Post-Pilot Iteration) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Counter checkout** | • `F2` search, `F3` quantity cycle, `F4` review, `Enter` confirm, `Escape` cancel, `F8` pending recovery.<br>• `submitting.current` ref + `readGeneration` prevent double-submit and stale background read overwrite.<br>• Checkpoint recovery survives crash/restart without duplicate invoice posting. | • **P2 (Deferred — Layout)**: Single long vertical page stacks Checkout, Workspace Draft, Inventory, Customers, Retail, Staff, Credentials, and Backup on one scroll canvas. Converting to top-level tabs (`Counter`, `Stock`, `Khata`, `Day Close`, `Admin`) will reduce scrolling once pilot data confirms operator habits.<br>• **P3 (Deferred — Copy)**: Header banner still says `Local review · 0.3.0` / `Use test data only`; keep for controlled pilot transparency, update before commercial GA. |
| **2. Barcode / code lookup** | • Exact SKU match takes priority over substring name matches on `Enter`.<br>• Key-repeat (`event.repeat`) and IME composition (`isComposing`) are ignored so sticky scanner `Enter` keys cannot double-fire.<br>• Scanning an existing cart SKU increments by `+1.000` unit automatically and checks stock ceiling. | • **P2 (Deferred — Feedback)**: When a weighable item (`kg`/`litre`) has stock `< 1.000` (e.g. `0.500 kg`), first add clamps cleanly to `0.500`, whereas a second barcode scan increments by `+1000` milli and blocks. Acceptable for barcoded unit packs; weighed items use `F3` quantity entry. |
| **3. Split payment** | • Enforces exact paise equality (`cash + upi + card + credit === totalPaise`) and requires a valid customer ID whenever `credit > 0`.<br>• Explicitly labels `UPI (unverified)` and `Card (unverified)` in preview, saved receipt (EN + PA), and `Payments & closing`. | • **P2 (Deferred — Convenience)**: Typing `600` into Cash on a `₹1,000` split requires manually typing `400` into the second tender rather than offering a one-click "Fill remaining `₹400.00`" helper or live remaining-balance badge. Deferred to avoid altering validated checkout DOM contracts right before pilot. |
| **4. Customer credit & repayment** | • Phone/name/ID lookup (`CustomerLookup`) populates checkout customer reference.<br>• Credit limit enforced natively (`credit_limit` refusal code).<br>• Printable statement escapes all customer text natively in Rust and re-verifies snapshot consistency right before `window.print()`. | • **P2 (Deferred — ID ergonomics)**: Creating a customer requires manually typing an alphanumeric `Customer ID` slug (`[A-Za-z0-9_-]+`, e.g. `cust-ramesh`) in addition to Customer Name and Phone. Auto-generating the slug from name/phone with optional override is high-value for post-pilot UX polish. |
| **5. Stock inward & manual stock adjustment** | • Durable two-step idempotent receipt (`Save item` / `Save stock change` -> `Acknowledge item save`) guarantees zero duplicate stock inward if the app closes mid-save.<br>• Blocks catalog edits while a checkout checkpoint is unresolved (`checkout_active`). | • **P2 (Deferred — Click count)**: Routine deliveries require clicking **Acknowledge item save** after every single SKU adjustment (`2` clicks per SKU instead of batch inward voucher). Safe for single-PC pilot accuracy; batch goods-inward bill entry should follow in v0.4. |
| **6. Returns / refunds** | • Native `quote_local_return` previews exact `Cash out`, `Credit reduction`, `Digital refund pending`, and `Pending payment canceled` before confirmation.<br>• Per-line condition (`Sellable`, `Damaged`, `Other — do not restock`) prevents damaged goods from polluting sellable stock. | • **P2 (Deferred — Invoice lookup)**: Manager must copy/paste or type the `Saved invoice ID` UUID into `Invoice ID for payment records` instead of clicking "Open in returns" directly from **Saved receipts**. |
| **7. Daily cash closing** | • Separates `Expected drawer cash` (cash sales + cash repayments + opening float − cash refunds) from unverified UPI/Card totals.<br>• Computes exact paise variance (`counted − expected`) and blocks backdated sales on a closed drawer (`day_closed`). | • **P3 (Deferred — Field placement)**: Shared `#retail-reason` input lives inside the lower return fieldset even when used for float/closing corrections; our P1 `requireReason()` fix now focuses `#retail-reason` automatically with a clear alert so operators are never stranded. |
| **8. Backup export & restore** | • Requires Owner password + independent 14–128 char Recovery passphrase, verifies ciphertext + SHA-256 on export, clears password inputs immediately, previews counts before restore, requires `RESTORE <12-hex>` phrase, writes mandatory pre-restore safety backup, and disables all staff accounts post-restore. | • **P2 (Deferred — Two-step password entry)**: Restore intentionally clears password inputs after **Verify and preview backup**, requiring the Owner to re-type passwords alongside `RESTORE <12-hex>` to confirm. High-friction by design for destructive point-in-time restore safety; keep as-is. |
